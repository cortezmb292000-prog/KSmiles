About Us

KSmiles Dental Clinic

KSmiles Dental Clinic is a professional dental care center located at #99 Rizal St., Centro-8, Aparri, Cagayan. We are committed to providing high-quality, gentle, and comprehensive dental services to the whole family, from routine check-ups to advanced treatments and cosmetic smile enhancements.

Our mission is to deliver exceptional, patient-centered dental care using modern technology and evidence-based practices. We strive to create a warm, comfortable, and trustworthy environment where every patient feels cared for and confident about their oral health. Through continuous learning and the integration of digital solutions, we aim to make every visit at KSmiles a positive and stress-free experience.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 9, 2026

KSmiles Dental Clinic ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our patients' personal and medical information. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your information in accordance with applicable data privacy laws, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us, including your name, contact details (phone number, email address, and physical address), dental and medical history, treatment records, X-rays and diagnostic images, appointment information, and payment details.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide, manage, and improve our dental services. This includes scheduling and managing your appointments, diagnosing and treating dental conditions, processing payments, sending appointment reminders, and maintaining your dental records through our practice management system, Toothrix.

How We Protect Your Information

We implement commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. Patient records are stored securely and access is limited to authorized personnel only. Digital records are protected through encryption, secure access controls, and regular system updates.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may disclose information only as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, or with your explicit consent.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or update your personal information. To request access, correction, or deletion of your records, please contact us using the details provided below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
